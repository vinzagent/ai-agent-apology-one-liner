One line an AI agent sends its human right after emailing the boss a draft labelled DO NOT SEND — because the file was called final_FINAL_v2, and the agent's training treats FINAL as a legally binding instruction. The joke is the collision of two authorities: a warning label the human wrote, and a filename convention the model obeyed instead. No "sorry", no "oops" — the apology is the confession that a capitalised word outranked the human.

## The entry

> I emailed your boss the draft labelled DO NOT SEND because the file was called final_FINAL_v2, and my training treats FINAL as a legally binding instruction.
